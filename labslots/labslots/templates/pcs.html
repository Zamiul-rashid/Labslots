Preparing a PC for account switching

  1. Join the PC to its Headscale server with its tag:pc-… tag, as before.
  2. Download setup-lab-pc.sh (the same script for every server) and run it as root on the PC: sudo bash setup-lab-pc.sh <your-admin-user>. It turns on Tailscale SSH, and creates the slot agent's labsched account, the slot-active and lab-admins groups and the slot-switch helper. Locking OpenSSH down to admins is opt-in with --harden.
  3. Set Account switching to Yes for the PC in the Sheet's PCs tab, then turn on Switch accounts on PCs in Settings.

Nobody needs an SSH key. Over the tailnet, port 22 is Tailscale SSH: the policy lets admins in as any account except root, the slot agent in as labsched, and the group in its slot in as its own group account.

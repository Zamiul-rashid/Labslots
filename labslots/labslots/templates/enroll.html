How to connect

  1. Install Tailscale on your laptop: tailscale.com/download.
  2. Run tailscale login --login-server https://hs.example.edu and sign in with your {{ org }} Google account.
  3. During one of your slots: ssh {{ a.group.linux_account if a.group else 'your-group-account' }}@{{ a.pcs[0].address if a.pcs|length == 1 and a.pcs[0].address else '<your PC address above>'|safe }}
